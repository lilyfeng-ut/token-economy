High-level method

Optimize in the right order.

The sequence matters. If you route before policy, compress before measuring quality, or cache before defining scope, local savings can create system-wide risk.

01 · AVOID

Should this call exist?

Use deterministic logic, exact reuse, semantic reuse, batching, or graceful rejection when inference adds no marginal value.

Quality floor remains non-negotiable
02 · SHRINK

What is the minimum sufficient context?

Select and compress evidence against a declared budget while preserving provenance, instructions, and critical facts.

Measure quality vs. tokens removed
03 · ROUTE

Which execution plan clears the bar?

Choose model, tools, retries, and verification jointly—based on expected total task cost, not the first-call price.

Route on predicted outcomes
04 · VERIFY

How much confidence is enough?

Match verification spend to impact and uncertainty. Escalate only when another pass is likely to change the decision.

Risk determines verification depth
05 · ATTRIBUTE

Did the spend create value?

Join cost and latency to task success, correction rate, user outcome, and an explicit business value proxy.

No savings metric without quality
06 · LEARN

What should change next?

Promote better routes, adjust thresholds, invalidate stale knowledge, and rebalance budgets through controlled experiments.

Version policy; preserve replay

The governing rule: cost optimization may choose among solutions that clear the quality and safety floor; it never sets that floor.
